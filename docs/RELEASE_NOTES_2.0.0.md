## Long-form — Marketing / club newsletter

**Fire FC 2.0 is live — the biggest update we've ever shipped.**

This release transforms Fire FC from "a great team app" into a complete club platform. If you're a parent, you'll feel a smoother registration and clearer communication with your coach. If you're a coach, you'll have real tools for managing tryouts and offers. If you're a club admin, you'll have everything you need to run your club — registration, payments, communications, compliance — in one place.

### For families

**A registration flow that actually feels good.** One account covers every kid in your family. Sign in with Google, Apple, or email — same login covers RSVPs, chat, push notifications, and your kids' profiles.

**Pay online.** Connect a card and pay your fee in seconds. Or split it into installments if your club allows that.

**Friendly nudges, not spam.** If you start a registration and don't finish, you'll get a gentle reminder. If a coach offers your kid a spot, you'll get a reminder before it expires. Each family gets each reminder at most once.

**Welcome videos from your coach.** When a coach sends your kid an offer, they can include a short personal video. Your kid sees their future coach before they even step on the field.

### For coaches

**A real tryout pool.** Every candidate in one filterable view. Favorite the ones you want, rate them 1-5 stars, leave notes the rest of your staff can see. No more text-message-and-spreadsheet workflow.

**Holds prevent poaching.** Place a 7-day hold on a candidate while you're deciding — other coaches can see it and back off.

**Offer letters with a few clicks.** Pick the team, position, jersey number, message body, and (optional) welcome video. The parent gets a unique link, opens it on any phone, taps Accept, and the player is on your roster.

**"Needs attention" filter.** Find candidates who've been in the pool for days with no favorites and no offers — the kids slipping through the cracks.

### For club admins

**A CRM for every player.** Profile view shows teams, guardians, registration status, payment history, attendance percentage, medical profile, forms checklist, admin notes, communication history, and a full activity timeline.

**Critical medical alerts at the top of every player view.** EpiPens, life-threatening allergies, active concussions without clearance — surfaced in red the moment you open the player. Structured fields for allergies, conditions with emergency action plans, medications, concussion log with return-to-play tracking, primary care doctor, and insurance.

**Bulk actions on registrations.** Select multiple and mark them paid, invite them to tryout, or email them at once.

**Customizable forms.** Define your own waivers and consents. Track who's signed and who's pending at a glance.

**Tiered pricing and coupons.** Set up early-bird windows, late fees, multi-tier pricing, and promo codes per registration product.

**Stripe Connect for direct deposit.** Funds go straight to your club's bank account. Fire FC never touches your money.

**Refunds — full or partial.** Issue them right from the player profile. Platform fees are reversed proportionally so you don't keep a cut on refunded transactions.

**Payment plans.** Split any registration fee into 2-12 installments with individual due dates. Each installment has its own payment link. The registration is marked paid once every installment is.

**Household unification.** When a family uses two different email addresses across kids, link them with one click. The family timeline rolls everything up into one view.

**Season lifecycle as a state machine.** Move seasons through Draft → Registration Open → Tryouts → Roster Locked → In Season → Ended → Archived. Every transition is logged with the admin's name and timestamp.

**Reports that matter.** Conversion funnel from submitted → paid → offered → accepted. Fee collection broken out by collected, owed, and processing surcharge. Coupon redemption table. Per-age-group registration counts.

**Weekly admin roundup email.** Every Sunday afternoon, each club admin gets a single email summarizing what needs their attention: candidates with no favorites, registrations still pending payment, offers expiring in the next 72 hours.

**Tasks system.** Keep admin todos out of your inbox. Assign tasks to yourself or other coaches, set due dates and priorities, link them to specific players.

### Smaller things you'll feel

• Weekly digest emails now show practice times in your local timezone (no more "3pm" when practice is at 9am — a real bug we just fixed)
• Push notifications for every meaningful event
• Faster app launch, smoother scroll, dozens of polish fixes
• Privacy: medical info and payment data are encrypted and access-controlled
