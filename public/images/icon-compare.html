What's different in the simplified mock:

  • No metallic outer rim (iOS provides its own rounded mask — the existing rim would clip)
  • Tactical board reduced to a single field circle + halfway line (less noise at small sizes)
  • Palette pulled to the brand: #c8202c crimson, #f5f3ee bone, #0d0d10 charcoal
  • Subtle drop shadow under the GK instead of heavy 3D bevels
  • Shield is a single bone stroke instead of a chrome outline + interior
